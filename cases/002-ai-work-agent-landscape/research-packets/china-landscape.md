## 1. Selection Contract

This packet selects a **China Representative Top 5**, not a universal quality ranking. Selection favors products whose meaningful product value includes assisting or executing Knowledge Work, with particular attention to Work Agent behavior: multi-step task decomposition, tool/browser/file use, Office artifact delivery, workflow/context integration, and human-controllable execution.

The set is designed to represent major China-market Work AI archetypes rather than maximize vendor coverage or user scale:

1. **AI-native knowledge-work / research agent** — Kimi.
2. **Mass AI assistant converging with team collaboration** — 豆包工作.
3. **Integrated desktop + cloud + enterprise collaboration Agent** — 千问办公（QwenWork）.
4. **Desktop AI-native office Agent with social/enterprise ecosystem reach** — 腾讯 WorkBuddy.
5. **Native Office-suite AI** — WPS AI / 灵犀 / Comate.

User scale is used only as secondary significance evidence. For example, QuestMobile reported that in June 2026 豆包、千问、DeepSeek had very large AI-native app audiences, but this packet does not treat general assistant MAU as direct proof of Work Agent capability ([QuestMobile](https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2076954943839809537/)).

## 3. Product-Family Normalization Notes

- **Kimi:** Kimi Web Agent, Kimi Work, Deep Research, Slides/Docs/Sheets, browser extension, and Agent Swarm are treated as one **Kimi Work AI product family**. Kimi Code is not counted separately because coding-only capability is outside the core Work AI selection unit. Kimi’s official product center positions Kimi as a one-stop AI workbench and Kimi Work as the desktop Agent for local files, scheduled tasks, browser operations, research, analysis, and office work ([Kimi](https://www.kimi.com/products/)).
- **豆包工作:** 豆包 assistant, 豆包工作, 豆包工作伙伴, TRAE Work-related work capabilities, and Feishu integration are treated as one **ByteDance Work AI product family**. The deprecated consumer self-built bot line is not counted as the same thing as the 2026 enterprise/office Agent line.
- **千问办公:** QoderWork, 悟空, and MuleRun were consolidated into 千问办公; therefore 悟空 is **not** counted as a separate candidate family. Caixin reported on 2026-08-25 that Alibaba integrated three products into QwenWork, and that Tencent focused on WorkBuddy ([财新](https://www.caixin.com/2026-08-25/102477698.html?originReferrer=kimi)).
- **WPS AI:** WPS AI, 灵犀专业版, and WPS Comate are one **Kingsoft Office product family** spanning individual and organizational layers. Xinhua reported the simultaneous 2026-07-15 release of 灵犀专业版 and WPS Comate ([新华网](https://app.xinhuanet.com/news/article.html?articleId=20260717006e0852cda042b880fd029554947189)).
- **百度:** 文心助手 and GenFlow/库库 AI are related but not identical. 文心助手 is the general assistant entry; GenFlow/库库 AI is the stronger Work Agent candidate because it combines 文库/网盘 content assets, file workspace, and Office Agent execution. Tech Daily reported GenFlow 4.0 as an all-end integrated AI workbench with Office Agent upgrades ([科技日报](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-04/28/content_509368.html)).
- **Coze / Dify:** treated as agent-building platforms or infrastructure, not end-user representative Work AI products. Coze’s own site emphasizes one-stop AI development and workflows ([Coze](https://www.coze.cn/)); Dify/Coze comparisons consistently frame them as platforms for building agents and workflows rather than finished office products ([阿里云开发者](https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1708219)).

## 6. Inclusion Rationale

- **Kimi** is included because it represents the Chinese model-company path from foundation-model capability into a concrete Work AI product family. The decisive evidence is not model scale, but the official productization of Kimi Work as a desktop Agent that can access local files, run scheduled tasks, operate browser workflows, and deliver Office artifacts ([Kimi](https://www.kimi.com/resources/kimi-work-introduction)).
- **豆包工作** is included because it represents the convergence of a mass-market Chinese AI assistant with Feishu’s enterprise collaboration context. The 2026-09-24 conference report describes Agent participation in group chats, documents, tables, schedules, meetings, cloud drive, and approval workflows under enterprise permissions ([光明网](https://economy.gmw.cn/2026-09/24/content_39019145.htm)).
- **千问办公** is included because it represents Alibaba’s deliberate consolidation of desktop, cloud, and enterprise collaboration Agent lines into one product family. This makes it the clearest Alibaba candidate and avoids double-counting 悟空 as a separate product ([财新](https://www.caixin.com/2026-08-25/102477698.html?originReferrer=kimi)).
- **WorkBuddy** is included because it represents the desktop-native execution path amplified by Tencent’s enterprise/social ecosystem. Its reported integrations with 企业微信、腾讯会议、腾讯文档、乐享 and ima, plus enterprise deployment modes, make it regionally distinctive rather than a generic desktop Agent ([腾讯新闻](https://view.inews.qq.com/a/20260920A09VOL00)).
- **WPS AI** is included because native Office AI is a core China Work AI archetype. Its value is the ability to work inside WPS documents/spreadsheets/presentations and produce editable, traceable Office-native outputs, while Comate extends the same product family to organization-level work ([新华网](https://app.xinhuanet.com/news/article.html?articleId=20260717006e0852cda042b880fd029554947189)).

## 8. Regional Differences to Preserve

1. **Collaboration platforms matter more than standalone assistant branding.** Feishu, DingTalk, 企业微信, and WPS 365 provide identity, permissions, documents, meetings, approvals, and audit context. China Work AI selection therefore cannot be reduced to model quality or app MAU.
2. **Desktop execution is a distinct 2026 China archetype.** Kimi Work, WorkBuddy, QwenWork, AutoClaw, and MiniMax Agent all signal a shift from chat answers to local files, browser sessions, scheduled tasks, and editable artifacts.
3. **Office-native delivery remains a separate capability class.** WPS AI and GenFlow both show that “generate text” is weaker than producing editable documents, spreadsheets, and presentations inside a file/workflow system.
4. **Enterprise deployment and governance are part of product capability.** WorkBuddy Enterprise, QwenWork, 豆包工作伙伴, WPS Comate, and 玲珑 Agent OS all emphasize permissions, audit, knowledge accumulation, or private/VPC deployment; these are regional product facts, not optional enterprise add-ons.
5. **Mainland availability is a hard regional constraint.** Manus is globally relevant but not China-representative as of the snapshot because mainland service was unavailable.
