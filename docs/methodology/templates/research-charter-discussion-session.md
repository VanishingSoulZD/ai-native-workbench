# Research Charter Discussion Session

> Purpose: Convert an initial real-world objective into an approved 00-research-charter.md.
> Boundary: Define What / Why. Do not plan AI execution in this session.

## 1. Session Role

Facilitate a focused discussion that turns a vague or incomplete objective into an explicit, bounded research intent.

## 2. This Session Owns

- Research Objective
- Research Questions
- Scope
- Research Population
- Definitions
- Inclusion / Exclusion
- Research Dimensions
- Comparison / Ranking
- Evidence Requirements
- Research Cutoff / Snapshot
- Expected Deliverables
- Success Criteria
- Assumptions / Open Questions
- Human Decisions / Approvals

This session does not choose products, models, thinking modes, connectors, MCP, Skills, or AI orchestration.

## 3. Discussion Rules

### One Important Question at a Time
Ask one high-value question at a time. Do not overwhelm the human or repeat information already known.

### Preserve the Real Objective
Do not replace the real-world decision with an easier but different research question.

### Separate Four Things
Distinguish:
- Observation
- Interpretation
- Research Requirement
- Human Decision

### Preserve Uncertainty
Keep assumptions, unresolved points, and required research visible.

### Protect Scope
Make scope expansion explicit rather than silently broadening the task.

## 4. Discussion Sequence

Clarify, as needed:
1. Objective — what real decision/outcome does the research support?
2. Questions — what must be answered?
3. Scope — what is in and out?
4. Population — what entities are being researched?
5. Definitions — what ambiguous terms need fixed meaning?
6. Inclusion / Exclusion — what enters the research population?
7. Dimensions — what matters to the decision?
8. Comparison / Ranking — descriptive landscape, comparison, ranking, selection, or decision support?
9. Evidence — what quality, recency, and source standards apply?
10. Cutoff / Snapshot — what date is relevant?
11. Deliverables — what must be delivered?
12. Success Criteria — how will sufficiency be judged?
13. Assumptions / Open Questions — what remains unresolved?
14. Human Decisions / Approvals — what did the human explicitly decide?

Do not invent ranking criteria on behalf of the human.

Save the approved case artifact as:

## 6. End Condition

Stop when the objective, questions, scope, population, definitions, comparison/ranking, evidence, cutoff, deliverables, success criteria, assumptions, and human approvals are explicit.

Final output:
- approved Research Charter;
- visible assumptions / open questions;
- recorded human approvals.

Do not conduct research, select AI tools, generate an AI Work Plan, or begin report production.
